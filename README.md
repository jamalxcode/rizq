# Rizq رزق

A Brogue-inspired roguelike set in modern Kuwait. You're young and ambitious, with a big dream. Head out from the Friday market through Shuwaikh's scrapyards, the Wafra farms and the winter camps into the deep desert. Reach **100,000 KD** net worth, then make it back through your front door.

**Play it at [rizq.sala.company](https://rizq.sala.company).**

The whole game is one HTML file. To play offline, open `index.html` in a browser.

## Features

- **26 depths across 5 zones:** The Neighborhood, Shuwaikh Industrial, Wafra Farms, The Winter Camps and The Deep Desert. Each zone has its own map generator, animals and hazards.
- **No experience points.** You get stronger from what you find:
  - **Training courses ♦** improve one item each, so choose carefully.
  - **Mom's machboos** raises your max energy.
  - **Protein shakes** raise your fitness.
- **Unidentified items:** drinks are unlabeled cans and bottles, and tips come as voice notes and diwaniya advice. Some are great, some are bad.
- **Herd economy:** barley wins over sheep, goats and camels. Dog treats turn a dog, fox, wolf or hyena into a loyal friend. Captive salukis and falcons can be untied. Your animals count toward your net worth.
- **Animals are driven off, never killed:** stray dogs, crows that steal cash, vipers, scorpions, camel spiders, Arabian wolves, hyenas and more.
- **Environment:** heat and thirst, drifting sandstorms, sabkha mud, soft dunes, and fire spreading through grass, palms and oil.
- **Kuwaiti touches:** chai in an istikana, gahwa from a dalla, a shemagh, a bisht, and a cousin you can call for help.
- **Deal rooms:** pick one offer and the others disappear.
- Auto-explore, click-to-travel, hover to inspect, a touch pad on phones, seeded runs and autosave.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Arrows / `hjkl yubn` / numpad | Move. Walk into an animal to shoo it. |
| `x` | Auto-explore |
| `>` / `<` | Travel to the way onward / back |
| `z` / `Z` | Wait a turn / rest until recovered |
| `i` | Bag |
| `t` | Throw |
| `g` | Pick up |
| `?` | Help |
