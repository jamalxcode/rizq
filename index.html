How to play

Explore each depth, pick up money and gear, and head for the way onward >. Deeper depths pay much more, and they're much more dangerous. When your net worth reaches 100,000 KD, come back up to your front door.

Traders wait by the way back on some depths: Bu Fahad's diwaniya, a garage, the farm market, a camp, and a Bedouin trader. Buy supplies and gear, sell animals (at 80% of their worth, or 130% for the day's request, up to three per trader), put cash in the bank where crows can't reach it, or call a pickup truck home. The truck costs 8% of your net worth and takes 15–25 turns to arrive, so you have to survive until it comes. Bring ma'amoul and they give you a better price.

No experience points. You get stronger from what you find. Training courses ♦ improve one item each, so choose carefully. Unknown drinks and tips are a gamble until you try them. Sleeping animals take triple damage if you sneak up on them.

Money: hand-feed dates to sheep and goats (one handful) or camels (two) to win them over. Animals only count toward your net worth while they're with you, so bring them home or sell them to a trader. Wolves and hyenas go after your herd. Dog treats turn a dog, fox, wolf or hyena into a loyal friend. Untie captive salukis and falcons. They're valuable. Watch your thirst, especially in the deep desert.
